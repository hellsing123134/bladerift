Horde survival

One hero against the Rift

Move with one thumb, your weapons fire on their own. Every level up is a choice, every run is a new build.

Survive the swarm

Hundreds of enemies pour out of the Rift. Dodge, kite them into a crowd and watch your build tear through it.

Chests, shrines, a travelling merchant and prisoners in crystals turn every run into a different story.

30-minute runsEndless modeRift tiers I-V

Bring down the bosses

Every rift has six bosses with their own attacks: shockwaves, ice cages, sandstorms, falling meteors.

A defeated boss drops a chest. Max a weapon, take its partner card, and the chest evolves it into something much bigger.

24 bossesWeapon evolutionsRift runes

A camp that grows

Between runs you come home to a floating island: a tavern, a workshop, pets by the well, townsfolk you rescued from the Rift.

Every boss you beat gets a stone statue in the garden.
